## August 2025
So this month I've decided that i want to try my best to get a **good** internship. \

Let me tell you that I've learnt a LOT. \
But when the time came to show my work online, it turned out that the internet is quite a dangerous place,
or at least that's whats the **reddit public opinion** is.
So I've decided on using a **VPS**  so if something goes terribly wrong my local network doesn't get compromised.
