  title: Information hoarding
---

We have the greatest library available at our fingertips.

It's a **great privilege**, and opportunity if we manage to *use it* instead of getting lured by the world of distractions and addictions.

But I don't want to talk about the addicted or distracted here.

I mean to talk about *the self-aware*. About those who want to take the best out of life and deepen their understanding.

I feel there is often a need in those individuals to maximise the value of their time spent. So they read ***a lot***. 

Gathering the latest news about neuroscience, state of the world, philosophy and news from their line of work in a week, only to forget it in a day. Funny thing is they probably **still feel they aren't learning *enough***.

We think we don't have enough time to delve deeper on the questions, beyond initial answers. We feel it's suboptimal to spend time trying to connect the dots to seemingly far away topics. Talking about trying to perform maintenance or revision of our knowledge **is beyond discussion.**

So we end up ***hoarding information***
