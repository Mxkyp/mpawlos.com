> **Me is a disease**. (Naval Ravikant)

Carrying around this *big* baggage - *the I*, of your beliefs of what political and societal group you belong to can be quite heavy no? Protecting oneself from pepole trying to infringe your character and status seems like a waste of energy. 

To be perfectly clear, I'm not pushing a kind of *relativity* to everything, to being shapeless and formless. \
Quite the contrary, I'm proposing not trying to hold an artificial image of yourself so the real one can live. **The one that shows from your actions, words and behaviours**. The one that changes constantly, not because of *changing circumstances* but ***evolving beliefs***.

> To define is to limit (Oscar Wilde)

Don't think about what person you are and what you want to be; **do**.
