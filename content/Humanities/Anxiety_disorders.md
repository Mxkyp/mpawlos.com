
[Anxiety and Phobia workbook by Edmund Bourne](./Anxiety_workbook_edmund_bourne.md)

He recognized four main types of anxiety disorders namely:

1. **panic attacks**
2. **agrophobia**
3. **social anxiety** and
4. **obsessive-compulsive disorder**

The example he provided for [**obsessive-compulsive disorder**](./Ocd.md)
about a driver which goes back just to make sure he hasn't hit anything,
for no apparent reason is something i can relate to.
I often experienced it too, and i often **feel a need to double or triple check** many things.
Also important [generalized anxiety](./Generalized_anxiety_disorder.md)
