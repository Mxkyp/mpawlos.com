## What it is

Its a general excessive fear appearing for example **when needs for performance are increased**. Accompanied often by physical symptoms such as **tension headaches and irratiable bowls symptoms**.

The cause of it could be parent **modeling worry behaviour**.

## treatment
1. Cognitive work - actively reform your self-talk habits. 
ex. remove the belief that **if you worry about it it won't happen**.
2. Reduce **worry behaviours** staring at the screen when you are stressed or taking a whole day off
3. Fear exposure and work, if you are scared about it, run to it.
4. Worry exposure - actively imagine yourself in your worst case scenario
