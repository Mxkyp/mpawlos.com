[Anxiety disorders](./Anxiety_disorders.md)

## what it is

It is anxiety caused by reoccurring [obsessions](./obsession.md) and furthered by [compulsions](./compulsion.md). 

## reflections
Its a potent combination because obsessions by themselves cause anxiety, that is
furthered down by the shame that comes with giving **through to compulsions**. So double anxiety(nice!).

## solutions
1. Understand that reoccurring thoughts are not yours, If some bad thought comes its just random noise not your wanting.
2. Response prevention, reduce the number of times you check, gradually.
