An **emotion** characterized by a feeling of tension, increased blood pressure.
Often **used interchangeably with fear** although there are not the same thing.

**fear** - short term, reasonable reaction to a threat \
**anxiety** - long term, future focused long acting

Works on physical psychological and behavioral levels. \
**physical** - increased blood pressure, tension, dizziness \
**psychological** - uneasiness, numbness, fear of dying, 
                going crazy or something going wrong. 
**behavioral** - prevent or force certain behaviors ie. \
             shyness, disturb doing everyday tasks
