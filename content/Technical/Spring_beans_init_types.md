# What are they?

Spring **[beans](./Spring_bean.md)** can be initialized in two ways.

1. Eagerly (after **[context](./Spring_context.md)** creation)
2. Lazy (with first reference)

**In the in the real world, not everything is possible due to other
factors like cost or time. If you cannot treat the real cause of the problem, you can
sometimes treat at least some of the symptoms.**
